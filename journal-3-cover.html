Amazon KDP Print Journal Cover Specifications

  • Trim Size: 6" × 9" (standard journal size)
  • Resolution: 300 DPI minimum for print quality
  • Color Mode: CMYK for print (or RGB converted to CMYK)
  • File Format: PDF recommended for upload
  • Bleed Area: 0.125" (37.5px @ 300 DPI) on all sides
  • Safe Area: 0.25" (75px @ 300 DPI) from edges - keep important content here
  • Page Count: Must include front cover, back cover, and spine
  • Font Embedding: Embed all fonts in PDF to ensure correct display
  • Margins: Content should stay within safe area to avoid cutoff
  • Spine Width Calculation: (Page Count × Paper Thickness) + Cover Thickness

Design Best Practices

  • Use high-contrast text for readability on printed materials
  • Test with KDP's cover previewer before final submission
  • Avoid thin lines or small text that may not print clearly
  • Ensure back cover has ISBN barcode placement area (2" × 2")
  • Maintain consistent branding and professional appearance
  • Use standard fonts (Georgia, Times New Roman, Arial, Helvetica)
