📋 Cover Specifications

Trim Size 6" × 9" (1800 × 2700px @ 300 DPI)
Resolution 300 DPI Minimum
Color Mode CMYK (or RGB to convert)
File Format PDF (Recommended)
Bleed Area 0.125" (37.5px) on all sides
Safe Area 0.25" (75px) from edges

📐 Complete Cover Dimensions

Total Width: Back Cover (6") + Spine (0.15" for ~200 pages) + Front Cover (6") = 12.15" (3645px @ 300 DPI)
Height: 9" (2700px @ 300 DPI)
Spine Width Note: Adjust based on page count: (Page Count × 0.0003") + 0.02"

✅ Pre-Submission Checklist

  • Front cover has eye-catching design and clear title
  • Back cover includes: description, benefits, barcode area (2" × 2")
  • Spine includes book title and is readable when spine faces up
  • All text is within safe area (0.25" from edges)
  • Resolution is 300 DPI minimum
  • File is PDF with embedded fonts
  • Color is CMYK or RGB (will convert to CMYK)
  • ISBN barcode area is clear on back cover (right side, bottom)
  • No important content extends into bleed area
  • Tested with KDP cover previewer

📝 Next Steps

  1. Customize text and colors to match your brand
  2. Add your author bio and photo to back cover
  3. Calculate exact spine width: (total pages × paper thickness) + cover thickness
  4. Export as PDF: Print → Save as PDF at 300 DPI
  5. Embed fonts before uploading to KDP
  6. Upload to Amazon KDP and use their cover previewer
  7. Review and approve in KDP dashboard
