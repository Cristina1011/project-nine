# Three Years of Us - Anniversary Website

## What's Ready:
✅ Beautiful dark-themed website (red & black, easy on the eyes)
✅ All poem sections and lyrics
✅ 50+ photo gallery
✅ Video section
✅ Responsive design
✅ Smooth animations

## What You Need to Add:
1. **Your Voice Recording** (poem-voice.mp3)
   - Record yourself reading "Three Years of You" poem
   - Save as MP3 or WAV
   - Place in the root folder as `poem-voice.mp3`

2. **Background Music** (optional for sections)
   - Extract audio from YouTube links if desired
   - Place as `background-music.mp3`

## How to Add Your Voice:
1. Record yourself reading the poem in a quiet space
2. Export as MP3
3. Rename to `poem-voice.mp3`
4. Place in `/home/user/project-nine/` folder
5. Refresh the website - it will automatically play during the poem section

## Website Features:
- Dark theme throughout (no bright lights)
- Red & black color scheme
- Smooth scrolling experience
- Photo gallery with hover effects
- Video player
- Audio player for your voice
- Responsive for all devices

## To View:
Open `index.html` in a web browser or deploy to a web server.
