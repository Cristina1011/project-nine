Three years of US

Mr. V, my devil—I want to tell you something. Today marks three years since you became my constant, and I need you to know what that means.

You've never been the type to speak your feelings out loud. Instead, you show me. You show me through the way you remember the small things, through the effort you put in.

August brought you into my life like a spark I didn't know I needed. But it wasn't the romance of new beginnings that captured me—it was how real you stayed.

You've been the one who actually does the work, who doesn't just promise but proves it day after day by actions. On my birthday, it wasn't flowers or words—it was your presence, your dedication to making me feel like I matter.

When winter wrapped around us, you didn't retreat. You built moments for us—creating a world for us where I felt safe, seen, and completely yours.

But what I'm most grateful for is how you've been there in the quiet moments. When I've been broken and couldn't hide it, you stayed. You held me. You didn't need words to fix me—your presence alone was the healing.

Three years, and you keep choosing. Not the best version of me, but all of me. The messy, scared, complicated me.

Forever your Angel, Forever your kitten.
